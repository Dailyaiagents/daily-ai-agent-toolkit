## Essay: Skills are procedural memory

**LinkedIn:** Reusable agent work needs more than a prompt copied from a transcript. A portable skill should preserve the method, its refusal conditions, examples, and a deterministic self-test. We wrote down what must be removed before an internal procedure becomes public. [HELD]

## Essay: Proof belongs inside the workflow

**X:** “Done” is not evidence. A useful receipt keeps artifact identity, method, expected condition, observed result, time, and limitations together—and is produced during the work, not assembled afterward. [HELD]

**LinkedIn:** Post-hoc proof is expensive and selective. The stronger pattern is to retain evidence as ordinary work moves from source to action to check to decision. The resulting receipt can block a release, narrow a claim, or request missing input. [HELD]

**Newsletter:** Subject: Proof belongs inside the workflow — A receipt should be a normal output of work, not a presentation assembled once everyone already wants to ship. [HELD]
